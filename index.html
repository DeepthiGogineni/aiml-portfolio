About me

I have about seven years of experience as a Salesforce developer, most recently building Agentforce AI agents and deployment pipelines. I am now studying AI and machine learning so I can design and judge the models behind the tools I build on.

Value proposition

I connect enterprise software experience with a working understanding of machine learning. I can explain an algorithm in plain words, say where it fits, and say where it does not.

Who this portfolio is for

Instructors and classmates reviewing my coursework, and hiring managers looking for people who can move between business platforms and machine learning concepts.
