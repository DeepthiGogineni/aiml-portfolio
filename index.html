Who I Am

A Little About Me

I am a Salesforce developer with 6 years of experience building customer service and workflow systems for large organizations. I write Apex and Lightning Web Components, design flows and OmniStudio guided processes, and connect Salesforce to other systems through REST, SOAP and MuleSoft. At Delta Air Lines I automated guided agent workflows with OmniStudio, set up a Copado deployment pipeline with automated tests and code review, and built Agentforce AI agents.

That work made me curious about what happens inside the AI features I was configuring. I am now completing a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence at Indiana Wesleyan University, where I am learning how machine learning models are built, tested and compared. I hold the Salesforce Certified Platform Developer I certification and a Master of Computer Applications from JNTU Kakinada.

I want to work where business platforms and machine learning meet: helping teams decide which AI approach fits a problem, and then building it well. I like explaining technical ideas in plain words, which is why this portfolio shows the reasoning next to the results. I live in Austin, Texas, and I am always glad to talk about enterprise automation and where AI in business software is going.
